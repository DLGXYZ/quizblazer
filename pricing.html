Questions

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The BlazerBox app and your first game are free.

Is there a subscription?

Subscriptions are coming soon. For now, you buy games as you need them, starting at $99 each, or buy 9 and get the 10th free.

Do players need phones or an app?

No. Players put their phones away and play on paper, just like a traditional trivia night.

What equipment do I need?

A laptop or Chromebook and a TV to mirror it to. That's it.

Do we need an internet connection?

No. BlazerBox is offline-capable, so the game runs even without Wi-Fi.

Do we need an experienced quizmaster?

No. The game pauses automatically and prompts your host, so any staff member or volunteer can run it.
