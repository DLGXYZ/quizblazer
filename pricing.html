Questions

Do players need to download an app?

No. Players join from their phone's browser by scanning a code.

What equipment do I need?

A phone or tablet for the host and a screen for the scoreboard. A dedicated venue display is optional.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time.

Can I write my own questions?

Yes. The Venue and Pro plans include the quiz builder for custom rounds.
