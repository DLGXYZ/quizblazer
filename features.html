Features

Everything you need to host a great trivia night

BlazerBox is a self-contained live trivia host. Here's what makes it special.

Setup

One laptop, one TV, done

BlazerBox runs the entire night from a single laptop or Chromebook, mirrored to your TV.

  • No special hardware to buy
  • Offline-capable, so it doesn't depend on your Wi-Fi
  • Everything the night needs lives on one machine

Production

A show, not a slideshow

Every round is fully produced so your night feels professional from the first question.

  • Real audio and video questions
  • Embedded royalty free music that sets the mood between questions
  • Professional pacing, so rounds never drag

Hosting

Any staff member or volunteer can host

The game pauses automatically whenever it needs the host and tells them what to do.

  • No quizmaster experience needed
  • Clear prompts for when to move the game on
  • Host enters team names and scores when asked

Play

Phones away, pens out

There's no player app. Teams watch the TV and play on paper, just like a traditional trivia night.

  • Nothing for players to download or sign up for
  • Keeps the room social and screen-free
  • Familiar for regulars, easy for newcomers

Scoreboard

Scores on the big screen

Once the host enters the scores, the standings go up on the TV for the whole room to see.

  • Team names shown on screen
  • Standings revealed as the night goes on
  • A proper finale for the winning team
