Features

Everything you need to host a great quiz

BlazerBox is built by quiz hosts, for quiz hosts. Here's what makes it special.

Play

Players join from their phones

Teams scan a QR code and join in seconds. No app download required for players.

  • Team names and avatars
  • Works on any smartphone
  • Answer locking so there's no second-guessing after time is up
Screenshot: player answer screen

Host

Run the whole night from one app

Start rounds, reveal answers and manage teams from the host controls.

  • Pause, skip or replay any question
  • Built-in timers per question or round
  • Joker rounds and bonus points
Screenshot: host control panel

Display

Live scores on your venue screens

Show questions, countdowns and the leaderboard on TVs and dedicated venue displays around the room.

  • Big, readable scoreboards
  • Round-by-round standings
  • Upcoming quiz nights shown between games
Photo: venue display in a pub

Create

Your quiz, your way

Use ready-made quiz packs or write your own rounds.

  • Picture, music and multiple-choice questions
  • Save and reuse your favourite rounds
  • Themed packs for holidays and special events
Screenshot: quiz builder

Grow

Bring people back every week

Turn one-off players into regulars.

  • Season leaderboards across multiple nights
  • Publish your quiz schedule
  • Results you can share on social media
Screenshot: season leaderboard
